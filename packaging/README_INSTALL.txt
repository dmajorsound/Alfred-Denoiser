Alfred - A simple Denoiser at your Service
dmajorsound.com

macOS
  Double-click install_mac.command. If macOS blocks it: right-click it, choose Open, then Open again.
  It copies Alfred to your user plug-in folders (VST3, Components, CLAP) and clears the download quarantine.
  Then restart REAPER or rescan the plug-ins.

Windows
  Copy Alfred.vst3 to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
  Copy Alfred.clap to C:\Program Files\Common Files\CLAP
  Then restart REAPER or rescan the plug-ins.
